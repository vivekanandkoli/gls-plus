Feature by feature

Walk through each module and note his feedback

Tx
Transactions
Daily operations
  • Every gold buy and sell is logged with weight, rate, client and an auto-numbered invoice.
  • Sellers see an estimated profit before they even submit the sale.
  • If a sale's rate looks far off from the true cost, the system flags it for a second look.
De
Deals
Daily operations
  • Bundles several transactions into one larger, wholesale-style trade.
  • Goes through the same admin approval before it counts.
Cl
Clients
Daily operations
  • A directory of every buyer and seller, with full transaction history and running totals.
  • Quick-select an existing client, or add a new one, right from the transaction form.
In
Invoices
Daily operations
  • Professional, printable invoices generated automatically from the transaction data.
  • Admin can correct an invoice number after the fact — the change is recorded.
Ap
Approvals
Oversight & insights
  • Staff submit; nothing counts toward stock or profit until an admin approves it.
  • Admin can reject with a reason — staff fix and resubmit.
  • Admin's own entries are approved automatically.
Wa
Cost engine (WAC)
Oversight & insights
  • Automatically works out the true average cost of the gold currently on hand.
  • Every sale's profit is measured against that true cost, not a guess.
  • Recalculates itself the moment any historical entry is approved, edited or removed.
Da
Dashboard
Oversight & insights
  • One glance shows current stock, this month's buy and sell volume, and profit.
  • Compares two profit views side by side: your own set rate versus the system's true-cost calculation.
  • Warns you when stock drops below the level you've set.
Re
Reports & statements
Oversight & insights
  • Accountant-ready monthly, per-client and annual statements.
  • Cash transactions are tracked separately and never mixed into official statements.
  • Everything can be printed or exported to Excel.
Us
User management
Oversight & insights
  • Create staff logins and assign them admin or staff access.
  • Deactivate anyone instantly — no server changes needed.
Ac
Activity log
Oversight & insights
  • Every change to every transaction is recorded — who did it, and when.
  • A complete paper trail if anything is ever questioned later.
