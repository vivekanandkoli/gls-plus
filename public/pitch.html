02
The Challenge

What gold traders face daily

Manual Excel tracking

Errors in running balance, duplicate invoices, no real-time stock view.

Slow invoice creation

Manually typed Thai/English invoices that don't match Thai Revenue Department format.

No business insights

No visibility into gross margin, client profitability, or year-on-year volume trends.

No client ledger

Hard to see total buy/sell history for any given client without scrolling Excel.

Stock discrepancies

Opening balance mismatches when importing new year data cause silent errors.

No mobile access

Excel files are desktop-only — no way to check rates or log trades on the go.

Module 1

Dashboard — at-a-glance view

7 live KPI cards

  • Current stock balance in grams
  • Stock value at latest sell rate (THB)
  • Total BUY volume & THB this period
  • Total SELL volume & THB this period
  • Gross margin (SELL − BUY cost) + margin %
  • Low stock alert when below threshold
  • Recent transactions list

Quick-add

★ One-click "New Transaction" button on the dashboard — no navigation required to start recording a trade.

Gross Margin card

◎ SELL revenue − BUY cost for the period. Color-coded: green = profitable, red = loss. Shows margin % alongside the THB value.
